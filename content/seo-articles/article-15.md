## Introduction

IONOS (formerly 1&1) is one of Europe's largest web services companies and a significant player in the domain registration market globally. Their marketing is hard to miss: bold promises of domains starting at $0.99 or even free with hosting packages. If you're considering registering a domain through IONOS, this **ionos domains review** will give you the full picture — including what happens after that cheap first year ends.

Spoiler: the first-year pricing is genuinely competitive. The renewal story is more complicated.

**Key Facts for 2026:**
- IONOS serves over 8 million customers across Europe and North America as of 2026
- IONOS .com domain renewals at ~$15/yr are 64% more expensive than Cloudflare's wholesale rate of ~$9.15/yr
- IONOS is one of the few registrars to offer 24/7 phone support — a genuine differentiator in 2026
- The 1&1-to-IONOS rebrand completed in 2018; the company's infrastructure and ownership remain the same under United Internet AG

## Company Background: What Is IONOS?

IONOS has been in the web hosting and domain business since the 1980s under various names, operating as 1&1 until rebranding to IONOS in 2018. They serve over 8 million customers across Europe and North America, primarily targeting small businesses and entrepreneurs.

Their product range extends well beyond domains — they offer web hosting, VPS and dedicated servers, website builders, email hosting, and Microsoft 365 plans. Domains are often used as a loss-leader to pull customers into the broader ecosystem.

## IONOS Domain Pricing: First Year vs. Renewal

This is the critical section of any **ionos domains review**. Let's look at the numbers honestly.

### First-Year Registration Prices

IONOS often runs promotional pricing that looks extremely attractive:

| Domain | IONOS First Year | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| .com | $0.99 – $1.00 | Promotional with hosting |
| .net | $0.99 – $1.00 | Promotional |
| .org | $0.99 – $1.00 | Promotional |
| .io | ~$25.00 | Standard rate |
| .co | ~$3.00 | Promotional |

These prices often apply when bundled with an IONOS hosting plan. Standalone domain registration may be priced differently.

### IONOS Renewal Pricing

After the first year, **ionos domain pricing** rises significantly:

| Domain | IONOS Renewal | Market Average | Premium Paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| .com | ~$15.00/yr | ~$13–14/yr | ~$1–2/yr |
| .net | ~$15.00/yr | ~$13–15/yr | Roughly equal |
| .org | ~$15.00/yr | ~$13–14/yr | ~$1–2/yr |
| .io | ~$39.99/yr | ~$30–35/yr | ~$5–9/yr |

The renewal prices aren't outrageously high — they're above average but not in GoDaddy or Network Solutions territory. The real issue is the gap between the $0.99 intro price and the $15/year renewal. If you're not prepared for that jump, it can feel like a bait-and-switch.

## Full Feature Review

### Domain Management Interface

IONOS has invested in its control panel interface in recent years. The current dashboard is cleaner than it was under the 1&1 branding. Key features include:

- DNS record management (A, CNAME, MX, TXT, SRV)
- Domain forwarding and masking
- Email forwarding
- Subdomain creation
- DNSSEC support (on eligible TLDs)

The interface is functional but not as intuitive as Namecheap or Cloudflare. Navigating between domain settings and hosting settings can feel disjointed — a common complaint when a company tries to manage too many products under one roof.

### WHOIS Privacy Protection

IONOS includes free WHOIS privacy protection on eligible domains. This is a positive — they haven't followed the GoDaddy model of charging extra for privacy. However, privacy availability depends on the TLD and plan type. Some lower-tier packages may have restrictions.

Always confirm that WHOIS privacy is enabled immediately after registration.

### DNS Hosting Quality

IONOS provides its own DNS hosting infrastructure, which is adequate for most users. DNS propagation is generally fast, and uptime has been reliable in third-party monitoring reports. That said, it doesn't reach the performance level of Cloudflare's DNS, which is widely considered the gold standard.

For users who want the fastest possible DNS resolution, pointing IONOS-registered domains to Cloudflare's name servers (which is free) is a recommended configuration.

### SSL Certificates

IONOS provides free SSL certificates with hosting plans, powered by Let's Encrypt or their own certificate authority depending on the plan. For domain-only registrations without hosting, SSL is not included — you'd need to source it elsewhere (which is standard practice).

### Email Features

IONOS offers built-in email hosting as part of various packages. For domain-only registrations, basic email forwarding is available. Full email hosting (actual inboxes) requires an additional package.

## The Transfer Strategy

If you registered a domain with IONOS for the low first-year price and are now approaching renewal, transferring is a legitimate option. Here's how:

1. Log in to IONOS and locate your domain in the domain section.
2. Unlock the domain (look for "transfer lock" or "domain lock" settings).
3. Request the EPP/authorization code — IONOS should email it to you.
4. Initiate a transfer at your new registrar (Namecheap, Porkbun, or Cloudflare).
5. Approve the transfer email when it arrives.
6. Wait 5–7 days for the transfer to complete.

The transfer itself typically costs one year's renewal at the new registrar — often $9–$14, still cheaper than IONOS's renewal rate.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is IONOS a good domain registrar?
IONOS is a competent domain registrar with a strong suite of associated services including web hosting, email, and website builders. Its first-year pricing is among the most competitive in the market, and it includes free WHOIS privacy on eligible domains. However, its renewal pricing (~$15/yr for .com) is above the market average, and its control panel is less polished than Namecheap or Cloudflare.

### What are IONOS domain renewal prices in 2026?
IONOS renews .com domains at approximately $15/year in 2026, compared to Cloudflare at ~$9.15/yr and Namecheap at ~$13.98/yr. IONOS renewal prices for .io are approximately $39.99/yr, which is significantly above average. First-year promotional pricing of $0.99 does not apply to renewals.

### How do I transfer a domain away from IONOS?
To transfer from IONOS: (1) log in and navigate to your domain in the IONOS control panel, (2) disable the transfer lock, (3) request your EPP/authorization code via email, (4) initiate a transfer at your new registrar using that code, (5) approve the confirmation email, and (6) wait 5–7 days for completion. DNS remains active during the transfer.

### Does IONOS charge for WHOIS privacy?
No. IONOS includes free WHOIS privacy protection on eligible domains, which is a positive compared to GoDaddy, which still charges for the same feature. However, privacy availability may vary by TLD and plan type — always confirm that WHOIS privacy is activated after registration.

### Does IONOS offer phone support for domain issues?
Yes. IONOS offers 24/7 phone support, which is rare among domain registrars in 2026. This is a genuine differentiator if you prefer resolving issues by phone rather than through live chat or email tickets. Response times and support quality vary, but the availability of phone support sets IONOS apart from Namecheap, Cloudflare, and Porkbun.

### Is IONOS the same as 1&1?
Yes. IONOS is the rebranded version of 1&1 Internet, which rebranded to IONOS in 2018. The underlying company, United Internet AG, remains the same. The infrastructure, data centers, and ownership did not change — only the brand name and some interface improvements were updated with the rebrand.

### Should I register my domain through IONOS or Namecheap?
For users who only need domain registration, Namecheap offers lower renewal pricing ($13.98/yr vs $15/yr for .com), a better control panel, and 24/7 live chat. For users who want hosting, email, and domain management under one roof with phone support, IONOS is a legitimate alternative despite the slightly higher renewal cost.
