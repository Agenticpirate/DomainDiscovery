## What Is Porkbun?

## What You Get for Free with Porkbun

This is where Porkbun truly differentiates itself. Most registrars charge for at least some of the following; Porkbun bundles them all for free:

### Free WHOIS Privacy
All eligible domains include WHOIS privacy at no extra cost. Your personal contact information stays off public databases, protecting you from spam and solicitation.

### Free SSL Certificates
Porkbun provides free SSL certificates through Let's Encrypt for domains pointed to compatible hosting. This is a genuine cost saver — SSL certificates can run $10–$100/year at competing registrars or hosts.

### Free Email Hosting
Porkbun includes basic email hosting: you can create a few email forwarders and even basic mailboxes at no additional cost. This is perfect for simple use cases like hello@yourdomain.com.

### Free DNS Management
Full control over your DNS records with an easy-to-use interface. Porkbun's DNS tools support all standard record types and propagate quickly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Porkbun a legitimate domain registrar?
Yes. Porkbun is an ICANN-accredited domain registrar that has been operating since 2015. It is headquartered in Portland, Oregon and has processed millions of domain registrations with no major reliability incidents. It maintains high ratings on review platforms like Trustpilot and is widely recommended by developers and domain professionals.

### How does Porkbun's pricing compare to Namecheap?
Porkbun is cheaper than Namecheap on renewals. Porkbun renews .com domains at $10.98/year versus Namecheap's $13.98/year. Porkbun also includes free SSL certificates, whereas Namecheap charges separately for SSL. Namecheap offers a wider TLD selection and 24/7 live chat support, which Porkbun's more limited support hours do not fully match.

### Does Porkbun include free SSL?
Yes. Porkbun provides free SSL certificates through Let's Encrypt for domains pointed to compatible hosting environments. This is included automatically at no additional cost, saving users $10–$100/year compared to SSL certificates purchased through competing registrars or hosting providers.

### Is Porkbun better than GoDaddy?
For most users, yes. Porkbun charges $10.98/year to renew a .com domain with free WHOIS privacy. GoDaddy charges $21.99/year for renewal plus $9.99 for privacy — a true annual cost of ~$31.98, which is nearly three times Porkbun's rate. GoDaddy's broader ecosystem (website builder, hosting, Microsoft 365) may justify the higher cost for some users, but for domain registration alone, Porkbun delivers significantly better value.

### What are Porkbun's support hours?
Porkbun offers live chat during business hours and email/ticket support that typically responds within a few hours. Unlike Namecheap (24/7 live chat) or GoDaddy (24/7 phone and chat), Porkbun does not offer around-the-clock live support. For most domain-related issues, their responsive ticket system and comprehensive knowledge base are sufficient.

### Can I transfer a domain to Porkbun from GoDaddy?
Yes. Transferring a domain from GoDaddy (or any other registrar) to Porkbun follows the standard ICANN transfer process: unlock your domain, request an EPP authorization code from GoDaddy, and initiate the transfer at Porkbun. The process takes 5–7 days and typically includes a one-year extension at Porkbun's renewal rate.

### Does Porkbun offer email hosting?
Yes. Porkbun includes basic email hosting at no extra cost, allowing you to create email forwarders and basic mailboxes (such as hello@yourdomain.com). This is suitable for simple personal or small business use cases. For more advanced email features, users may prefer a dedicated email service like Google Workspace or Zoho Mail.
