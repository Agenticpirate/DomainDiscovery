## What Is Cloudflare Registrar?

Cloudflare Registrar is an ICANN-accredited domain registrar that operates on a fundamentally different business model from most of its competitors. Rather than marking up the wholesale cost of domains to generate profit, Cloudflare passes the cost directly to you.

The result is that Cloudflare domains pricing is typically among the lowest available for supported TLDs — especially on renewals, where most registrars make their real money.

**Key differentiators:**
- No markup on domain pricing (at-cost)
- Free WHOIS privacy on all domains
- DNS powered by Cloudflare's global anycast network (one of the fastest in the world)
- No upsells, no promotional pricing tricks
- Integrated with Cloudflare's broader suite of security and performance tools

## Cloudflare Registrar Limitations

No registrar is perfect, and Cloudflare Registrar has some important limitations to understand before you commit:

### Limited TLD Selection
Cloudflare supports a narrower range of TLDs compared to registrars like GoDaddy or Namecheap. Popular extensions like .com, .net, .org, .io, .dev, and .co are supported, but many country-code TLDs and newer gTLDs are not available.

### No Outbound Domain Transfers (Historically)
This is the biggest limitation. Historically, Cloudflare did not support outbound domain transfers — meaning once you moved a domain to Cloudflare, you could not easily move it to another registrar. Cloudflare has been working on this limitation, and partial support now exists, but it remains more complex than a standard transfer.

### Requires a Cloudflare Account
To register a domain with Cloudflare Registrar, you must have a Cloudflare account and the domain must be added to that account. This is not a burden for existing Cloudflare users, but it adds a step for newcomers.

### No Domain Auctions or Aftermarket
Cloudflare does not have a domain marketplace or auction platform. If you want to buy or sell premium domains, you will need to use a different registrar or aftermarket platform.

### No Email Hosting
Cloudflare does not offer email hosting. You will need to manage email separately (Google Workspace, Zoho, Fastmail, etc.).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Cloudflare Registrar the cheapest domain registrar?
Yes. Cloudflare Registrar is the cheapest reputable domain registrar for .com domains in 2026, charging approximately $9.15/year at wholesale cost with no markup. This price is the same for registration and renewal. Spaceship and Porkbun are close alternatives at $10.99 and $10.98/year respectively, but Cloudflare's flat at-cost pricing remains the industry benchmark for lowest price.

### Can I transfer my domain away from Cloudflare?
Outbound domain transfers from Cloudflare are more complex than standard transfers at other registrars. Cloudflare has been improving this capability, and partial outbound transfer support exists, but it requires more steps than a typical ICANN transfer. Users who anticipate wanting to switch registrars in the future should consider this lock-in risk before moving domains to Cloudflare.

### Do I need a Cloudflare account to use Cloudflare Registrar?
Yes. To register or transfer a domain to Cloudflare Registrar, you must have a Cloudflare account and add the domain to it. Cloudflare accounts are free to create and provide access to the full range of Cloudflare products. For users already using Cloudflare for CDN, DNS, or security, this requirement adds no friction.

### Does Cloudflare Registrar include free WHOIS privacy?
Yes. Cloudflare Registrar includes free WHOIS privacy protection on all eligible domains. Your personal contact information is automatically masked in public WHOIS databases at no additional cost.

### Is Cloudflare Registrar good for beginners?
Cloudflare Registrar is generally not recommended for beginners. It requires a Cloudflare account, has a DNS-centric interface that assumes technical familiarity, lacks live customer support, and has a more complex outbound transfer process. For beginners, Namecheap or Porkbun offer similarly low pricing with more accessible interfaces and better support options.

### What TLDs does Cloudflare Registrar support?
Cloudflare Registrar supports a limited selection of TLDs including .com, .net, .org, .io, .co, .dev, .app, and a smaller number of other extensions. It does not support as wide a range of country-code TLDs or newer gTLDs as registrars like GoDaddy or Namecheap. Users needing specific or less common TLDs should verify availability before committing to Cloudflare.

### Does Cloudflare offer domain email hosting?
No. Cloudflare Registrar does not offer email hosting. Users who need email at their domain must set up email separately using a service like Google Workspace, Zoho Mail, Fastmail, or another email provider. Porkbun is the registrar most directly comparable to Cloudflare on price that does include basic email hosting for free.
