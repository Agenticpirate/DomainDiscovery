## Pricing Comparison

### Registration Costs

Namecheap has historically been one of the most competitive registrars for first-year registration prices. A .com domain typically runs around $9–$10 in the first year, often with coupon codes pushing it even lower.

Google Domains (now Squarespace) was always straightforward — $12/year for a .com, no tricks, no introductory pricing. What you saw was what you paid, year after year.

**Winner: Namecheap** for initial registration if you're budget-conscious.

### Renewal Costs

This is where the **google domains review** landscape gets interesting. Google Domains always prided itself on flat, transparent pricing. At $12/year renewal for .com, you never had to worry about a bait-and-switch.

Namecheap's renewal price climbs to around $13–$14/year for .com after the first year. It's still competitive, but it's not as flat as the Squarespace/Google Domains approach.

**Winner: Squarespace (Google Domains)** for renewal price consistency.

---

## Privacy Protection

### Namecheap WhoisGuard

Namecheap offers free WHOIS privacy protection through its WhoisGuard service. This hides your personal information — name, address, phone number, email — from the public WHOIS database. For most domains, this is completely free, which used to be a major differentiator when many registrars charged $10–$15/year for privacy.

### Google Domains / Squarespace Privacy

Google Domains also included free WHOIS privacy from day one. Squarespace has maintained this policy. Both registrars are tied here — privacy protection is free and automatic.

**Winner: Tie** — both offer free WHOIS privacy protection.

---

## DNS Management

Both registrars provide solid DNS management interfaces. Namecheap's Advanced DNS panel lets you add A, CNAME, MX, TXT, SRV, and AAAA records with ease. It also supports URL redirects and dynamic DNS.

Squarespace's DNS management (inherited from Google) is clean and minimal — great for beginners but somewhat limited for power users who want granular TTL control or advanced record types.

If you're running complex setups — multiple subdomains, mail servers, or CDN configurations — Namecheap's interface gives you more control.

**Winner: Namecheap** for advanced DNS users.

---

## Domain Transfer Experience

Transferring a domain away from a registrar is sometimes necessary. Namecheap makes outbound transfers relatively painless, though the 60-day lock after certain changes applies (this is an ICANN requirement, not specific to Namecheap).

The Squarespace acquisition of Google Domains complicated transfers for some users. Some reported confusion during the migration window. By 2026, the process has stabilized, but Namecheap's transfer tools remain more polished.

**Winner: Namecheap** for ease of domain transfers.

## Customer Support

### Namecheap Support

Namecheap offers 24/7 live chat support, which is a significant advantage. Their knowledge base is extensive, and response times are generally fast. Phone support is not offered, but the live chat more than compensates.

### Squarespace (Google Domains) Support

Squarespace support is available via email and live chat, but availability and response times are less consistent than Namecheap. Google Domains was famously support-light — mostly community forums and documentation.

**Winner: Namecheap** for customer support.

## Additional Features

### Email Hosting

Neither registrar offers full email hosting, but both provide email forwarding. If you need actual inboxes (e.g., you@yourdomain.com), you'll need to connect a service like Google Workspace or Zoho Mail regardless of which registrar you choose.

### Domain Parking and Forwarding

Both registrars support URL forwarding and domain parking. Namecheap has a slightly more configurable parking page system.

### Bulk Domain Management

Namecheap wins significantly here. If you manage multiple domains — for clients, projects, or a portfolio — Namecheap's bulk search, bulk registration, and bulk renewal tools are far superior to what Squarespace offers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Google Domains still available in 2026?
Google Domains was sold to Squarespace in 2023 and no longer exists as a standalone product. In 2026, domains previously managed through Google Domains are now under Squarespace Domains. Existing customers were migrated automatically, and new registrations go through Squarespace's domain platform.

### Which is cheaper, Namecheap or Google Domains?
Namecheap is cheaper for first-year registration at approximately $9.98/yr for .com versus Squarespace's $12/yr. However, Squarespace offers more predictable renewal pricing at $12/yr, while Namecheap renews .com domains at around $13.98/yr. Over multiple years, Squarespace's flat pricing can be slightly cheaper for renewals.

### Does Namecheap include free WHOIS privacy?
Yes, Namecheap includes free WHOIS privacy through its WhoisGuard service on most TLDs including .com, .net, and .org. This protection masks your personal contact information in the public WHOIS database at no additional cost.

### Can I still transfer my Google Domains domain to Namecheap?
Yes. Domains migrated from Google Domains to Squarespace can be transferred to Namecheap or any other registrar. The process involves unlocking the domain in your Squarespace account, requesting an authorization code, and initiating the transfer at Namecheap. The process typically takes 5–7 days.

### Is Namecheap a reliable registrar?
Namecheap is one of the most established domain registrars globally, having operated since 2000 and managing over 17 million domains. It maintains strong uptime for DNS services, offers 24/7 live chat support, and has a long track record of customer satisfaction for domain registration and management.

### What is the best domain registrar for beginners?
For beginners who prioritize simplicity, Squarespace Domains (formerly Google Domains) offers a clean interface with minimal configuration required. For beginners who want better pricing with only slightly more setup, Namecheap is an excellent choice and offers extensive documentation and 24/7 support.

### Does Namecheap offer bulk domain registration?
Yes, Namecheap offers robust bulk domain tools including bulk search, bulk registration, and bulk renewal. These features make Namecheap particularly well-suited for agencies, developers, and portfolio domain investors managing 10 or more domains simultaneously.
