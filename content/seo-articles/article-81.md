> **Quick Answer:** Afternic charges 20% commission but offers unmatched distribution through 100+ partner registrars including GoDaddy. Sedo charges only 15% commission and adds domain parking revenue plus strong international reach. For most sellers, listing on both platforms simultaneously delivers the best Afternic vs Sedo results.

If you own a valuable domain name and you're ready to sell, you've likely landed on the same two names everyone else does: Afternic and Sedo. These platforms dominate the aftermarket domain space, and for good reason — both offer massive buyer networks, brokerage services, and years of proven sales history. But in the Afternic vs Sedo debate, which one actually puts more money in your pocket?

This domain marketplace comparison breaks down both platforms across the metrics that matter most: fees, buyer reach, listing tools, and ease of use. By the end, you'll know exactly which platform fits your selling strategy.

**Key Facts for 2026:**
- Afternic distributes domain listings to 100+ partner registrars, including GoDaddy — the world's largest registrar with over 84 million domains under management
- Sedo has facilitated over $500 million in domain sales since its 2001 founding and maintains listings across 200+ countries
- The 5% commission difference between Afternic (20%) and Sedo (15%) equates to $500 extra per $10,000 sale on Sedo
- Cross-listing on both platforms is permitted and costs nothing extra — portfolio sellers report 15–30% more inquiries when using both

## What Is Afternic?

Afternic is a domain marketplace owned by GoDaddy, the world's largest domain registrar. It was founded in 1999 and has since built one of the most extensive distribution networks in the industry. When you list a domain on Afternic, it can appear across more than 100 partner registrars, including GoDaddy itself, Namecheap, and Network Solutions.

### Key Features of Afternic

- **Distribution network**: Your domain listing appears on partner registrar searches automatically
- **Fast Transfer domains**: Domains registered at GoDaddy or transferred to Afternic can be sold and transferred in minutes
- **Premium listings**: Afternic promotes high-value domains in front of buyers actively searching
- **Integrated pricing tools**: Sellers get suggested pricing based on comparable sales data
- **Brokerage service**: For high-value domains, Afternic offers negotiation support

Afternic's biggest strength is its GoDaddy integration. Because GoDaddy powers a massive share of global domain searches, your listing gets visibility at the exact moment buyers are looking for names.

---

## What Is Sedo?

Sedo (Search Engine for Domain Offers) is a German-founded marketplace launched in 2001. It operates independently and has built its own international buyer network across more than 200 countries. Sedo handles over 18 million domains in its database and has facilitated some of the largest domain sales in history.

### Key Features of Sedo

- **International reach**: Particularly strong in European markets
- **Sedo Brokerage**: Active broker team that negotiates on your behalf
- **Domain parking**: Earn revenue from parked domains while they're listed for sale
- **Auction system**: Run time-limited auctions to create urgency among buyers
- **SEO-friendly listings**: Sedo's marketplace pages are indexed by search engines, driving organic buyer traffic

Sedo's parking program is a standout feature. While your domain sits for sale, Sedo displays relevant ads on it and shares the revenue with you. For high-traffic domains, this can generate meaningful income while you wait for the right buyer.

## Afternic vs Sedo: Fee Comparison

Fees are often the deciding factor in a domain marketplace comparison. Here's how both platforms stack up.

### Afternic Commission Rates

- **Standard listing**: 20% commission on sale price
- **Premium listing**: 20% commission (same rate, higher visibility)
- **GoDaddy-registered domains**: Commission may vary depending on listing tier

Afternic's 20% commission applies regardless of whether the sale happens through their website or a partner registrar network. There's no listing fee to publish your domain.

### Sedo Commission Rates

- **Standard sales**: 15% commission (minimum $60)
- **Brokered sales**: 15% commission
- **Domain parking**: Revenue share varies by traffic

Sedo's 15% commission is lower than Afternic's 20%, which makes a meaningful difference on high-value sales. On a $10,000 domain, that's $500 extra in your pocket with Sedo.

## Buyer Reach: Who Finds Your Domain?

Both platforms offer impressive reach, but through different channels.

### Afternic's Distribution Network

Afternic wins on sheer distribution volume. When a buyer searches for a domain at GoDaddy and the name is taken, GoDaddy suggests Afternic listings as alternatives. This passive, high-intent traffic is extremely valuable. Buyers don't need to visit Afternic.com — they find your listing inside the registrar's own interface.

### Sedo's Organic and Direct Traffic

Sedo attracts buyers who are specifically hunting for premium domains. Many experienced domain investors and businesses search Sedo directly. Its international reach also gives you access to European and Asian buyers who may not use GoDaddy as their primary registrar.

**Verdict**: For U.S.-focused domains and names tied to popular English keywords, Afternic's GoDaddy network gives you a major edge. For international domains or names with multilingual appeal, Sedo's global buyer pool is more valuable.

## Listing and Management Tools

### Afternic's Seller Dashboard

Afternic's interface is functional but straightforward. You can set your price, choose between Buy Now or Make Offer options, and view inquiry activity. The Fast Transfer badge (exclusive to GoDaddy-registered domains) significantly speeds up the closing process.

### Sedo's Seller Dashboard

Sedo offers more detailed analytics on listing performance, including views and click data. You can also configure parking pages, run auctions, and access Sedo's brokerage team directly from the dashboard. The platform feels more seller-focused overall.

## Which Platform Is Better for Different Sellers?

### Best for Beginners: Afternic

If you're new to domain selling and your domains are registered at GoDaddy, Afternic is the simpler and more integrated choice. Setup takes minutes, and the passive distribution network does the marketing for you.

### Best for Portfolio Sellers: Sedo

If you have a large portfolio and want to maximize revenue per sale, Sedo's lower commission rate and parking revenue make it the stronger financial play.

### Best for Premium Domains: Both

For domains valued above $10,000, it's worth listing on both platforms simultaneously. Most sellers do exactly this — cross-listing increases exposure without much additional effort.

## Expert Verdict

Afternic and Sedo represent two fundamentally different philosophies for domain sales. Afternic is a distribution-first platform — its GoDaddy integration ensures your domain surfaces at the exact moment high-intent buyers are searching at the world's most popular registrar. This passive reach is difficult to replicate and explains why Afternic consistently drives high sales volume for .com domains with popular English keywords. The 20% commission is the price of that distribution engine.

Sedo, by contrast, is a marketplace-first platform that rewards sellers who want more control, lower commissions, and income from parked assets. For large portfolio holders managing dozens or hundreds of domains, Sedo's 5% commission advantage compounds meaningfully. A seller moving $200,000 in domain inventory annually saves $10,000 in commission by using Sedo instead of Afternic — a figure that far exceeds any productivity or convenience benefit.

The authoritative recommendation for serious domain sellers in 2026 is unambiguous: list on both. The platforms are free to list on, cross-listing is explicitly permitted, and the incremental effort is minimal compared to the exposure gains. Prioritize GoDaddy-registered domains on Afternic for Fast Transfer advantages, and use Sedo's brokerage team for your premium and internationally-appealing names.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Afternic or Sedo better for selling a .com domain?
Afternic has the edge for .com domains, particularly those with popular English keywords. Its distribution through GoDaddy's network means your listing appears to millions of buyers searching for domain names at the point of purchase. However, for premium .coms above $10,000, both platforms should be used simultaneously.

### Can I list my domain on both Afternic and Sedo at the same time?
Yes. Cross-listing on multiple domain marketplaces is permitted and common practice. Listing on both Afternic and Sedo simultaneously increases your domain's exposure without additional cost, since both platforms are free to list on. Just ensure your pricing is consistent across both to avoid confusion.

### What is Sedo's minimum commission fee?
Sedo charges a minimum commission of $60 per transaction, even if 15% of the sale price would be less than that amount. This makes Sedo's economics less favorable for very low-priced domain sales (under $400), where the minimum fee effectively represents a higher percentage.

### Does Afternic charge listing fees?
No. Afternic charges no upfront listing fee. The 20% commission is deducted only when a domain sells. This means listing large portfolios on Afternic costs nothing until a sale occurs, making it a risk-free option for sellers building their first domain portfolio.

### How long does it take to sell a domain on these platforms?
Timelines vary widely. Hand-registered domains may sell in weeks or take years depending on demand. Industry data suggests the average domain sits listed for 6–18 months before selling. Premium domains with strong keywords tend to sell faster. Afternic's passive distribution can accelerate sales by reaching buyers who weren't actively looking at a marketplace.

### Does Sedo's domain parking actually generate revenue?
Sedo's parking program generates meaningful revenue only for domains with existing organic traffic. A domain receiving hundreds of monthly visitors can earn $20–$200+ per month through parking. For newly registered domains with no traffic, parking income is negligible. The program is most valuable for aged domains with established backlinks or direct type-in traffic.

### What is the best domain marketplace for international buyers?
Sedo is definitively stronger for international sales. Its 200-country buyer network and particularly deep penetration in European markets make it the preferred marketplace for sellers with domains that have multilingual appeal or country-code TLDs. Afternic's strength is concentrated primarily in English-language and U.S.-focused domains.
